About the SMURF Assessment Tool:

  • The Smart Buildings Readiness (SMURF) Assessment Tool enables the evaluation of the Smart Readiness Indicator (SRI) across different upgrade scenarios by considering the potential integration of smart technologies.
  • It provides quantified assessments of each scenario by estimating the impact of specific upgrades on the SRI score, including changes in functionality levels and the use of advanced building systems such as smart thermostats, automated controls, and monitoring equipment.
  • In addition to technical evaluation, the tool presents the estimated cost of each proposed upgrade, supporting a clear comparison of cost-effectiveness between alternatives.
  • SMURF is designed to assist building owners and users, facility managers, energy auditors, and other stakeholders in making informed decisions during construction or renovation, aiming to maximize SRI gains while managing investment levels.
